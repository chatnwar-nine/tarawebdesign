Everything below follows from one idea. Vael does the work. You keep control.

CFOs, controllers and accountants at companies from $10M to $1B in revenue.

03

The character

Vael has a face so people know an agent is at work. The face shows what Vael is doing. It never decorates.

Eye · 10% × 22% of Ø Gap · 16% of Ø Highlight at 34% / 28%

Anatomy

Body
Neon sphere. Radial shade to #A9C200 at the edge.
Eyes
Two Ink pills. Centre at 46% height.
Mouth
None. Ever. Eyes carry the emotion.
Glow
Neon at 35%, blur half the diameter. Large sizes only.
Sizes
24 · 30 · 72 · 96 · 128 px

Behaviour

Gaze
Eyes follow the cursor. Max shift 14% × 10% of Ø.
Blink
130 ms, every 2.5 – 6.5 s, random.
Breath
Scale 1 → 1.035, 4.5 s loop.

States

Ready

Waiting for a task. Looks at you.

Thinking

Eyes look up and away. Planning.

Working

Eyes narrow. A point orbits.

Done

Eyes smile. One small hop.

Do

  • Show the face next to what Vael says or does.
  • Match the state to the real task status.
  • Use one face per card or screen.

Don't

  • Add a mouth, arms, hats or props.
  • Recolour the body.
  • Use the face as decoration or a mascot sticker.
  • Show it below 24 px.

06

Voice

Write like a senior accountant who respects your time. Short. Plain. Exact.

Grade 5–7

Short words. Short sentences. If a 12-year-old can't read it, rewrite it.

Active voice

"Vael matched 4,690 lines." Not "4,690 lines were matched."

No fluff

Cut adverbs and qualifiers. Swap adjectives for numbers.

Who speaks

Vael · in the product

"I drafted 6 journal entries. Nothing posts until you approve."

First person. Says what it did, then what it needs from you.

The company · in marketing

"Your books close while you sleep."

Talks to "you". Leads with the outcome, then the proof.

Rewrite it

Instead ofWrite
Leverage AI-powered automation to streamline your financial close process.Close your books in 2 days, not 9.
Our cutting-edge platform seamlessly integrates with your existing tools.Vael works with NetSuite, Stripe and 40 more. Live in a day.
Transactions have been successfully reconciled.I matched 4,690 lines. 122 need a look.
Revolutionary, game-changing, next-gen.A number.
07

Imagery

No stock photos. We show the product, the data and the light.

Do

  • Use one light source, always Neon.
  • Keep generous dark space around art.
  • Use precise numbers in UI shots: $12,400, not $XX.

Don't

  • Robots, brains, circuit boards or glowing blue.
  • People pointing at screens.
  • 3D blobs in many colours.
